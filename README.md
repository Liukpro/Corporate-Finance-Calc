# Corporate Finance Calc

## What this project does

A web-based tool (Streamlit) that calculates standard financial metrics from manual inputs. No complex automation, no Excel parsing - just transparent formulas and immediate results.

---

## Calculation logic

All formulas are implemented as pure functions in `formulas.py` (historical analysis, ex post) and `analisi_prospettica_area_caratteristica.py` (prospective analysis, ex ante), with:

- **Multiple calculation paths** - each metric can be computed in different selectable ways (e.g., FCCNOgc from Revenue/Costs or from MOL)
- **Explicit error handling** - division by zero and insufficient data raise exceptions
- **Session state** - the app stores previous calculation results (ex-ante results are stored under separate keys, so they never overwrite the ex-post ones)
- **Demo on Streamlit:** https://corporate-finance-calc.streamlit.app/
### **Warning**: to access the demo press "Yes, get this app back up!" after entering the given url.

### Current features

| Area | What it calculates |
|------|---------------------|
| Cash flows (ex post) | FCCNOgc, FCGC, FCID, FCFR, FCRf, FCU, FCE, Liquidity variation |
| Prospective Analysis (ex ante) | FCGC per period (net operating margin, depreciation tax shield, working capital changes), FCID per period (outlays, incremental costs, opportunity cost of reused assets, final disposal net of capital gain/loss taxes), FCU = FCGC + FCID |
| Cost of capital | WACC |
| Profitability ratios | ROS, ROI, ROE |
| Investment valuation | NPV with FCU (discounted at WACC) or FCE (discounted at Ke), with fixed periodic costs. The FCU-based NPV can use the ex-ante FCU directly (flows from t=0, initial investment included) or manual inputs |
| Bonds | Zero coupon (value and YTM), Coupon bonds |
| Stocks | Gordon growth model, No-growth model, VAOC |
| Mortgage | Italian (constant capital share) and French (constant payment) amortization schedules |

### Metrics' Details

| Metric | Full name | What it represents |
|--------|-----------|--------------------|
| **FCCNOgc** | *Flusso di Cassa Circolante Netto Operativo della Gestione Caratteristica* | Cash generated by core operations **before** changes in working capital and after income taxes. |
| **FCGC** | *Flusso di Cassa della Gestione Caratteristica* | Cash actually generated (or absorbed) by core operations, after working capital changes. |
| **FCID** | *Flusso di Cassa degli Investimenti/Disinvestimenti* | Cash flows from buying and selling fixed assets. |
| **FCFR** | *Flusso di Cassa dei Finanziamenti/Rimborsi* | Cash flows from raising and repaying financing (debt and paid-in equity). |
| **FCRf** | *Flusso di Cassa delle Remunerazioni finanziarie* | Cash paid to providers of capital: interest expense and dividends. |
| **Liquidity variation** | *Variazione di liquidità* | Net change in cash over the period: the sum of the four areas. |
| **FCU** | *Flusso di Cassa Unlevered* | Cash flow of operations and investments, independent of how the firm is financed. |
| **FCE** | *Flusso di Cassa agli Equity* | Cash flow available to shareholders, after financing flows. |

Note: This is a calculator, not an automated analysis engine. All data must be entered manually. There is no cross-validation or automatic consistency checking.

## System Status and Other Important Information, read carefully.

This project is not a finished application, results may be subject to errors. Do not rely only on the data shown.

Notes on the prospective analysis (work in progress):
- Depreciation of each outlay starts the period after its payment year. If the depreciation life extends beyond the project life, the quotas after the last period are not deducted in the FCGC; the residual book value is accounted for at disposal in the FCID.
- The NPV page reads the ex-ante FCU from the session: if you change the inputs in the ex-ante page, recalculate FCGC, FCID and FCU before going back to the NPV.
- The ex-post FCU is not linked to the NPV page (it is a single past value, not a series of future flows): enter it manually if needed.
